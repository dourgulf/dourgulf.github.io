English

Game data

MeowTrails is a logic puzzle game. It does not require an account. Game progress, board state, tutorial progress, and preferences are stored on your device. The current app does not send this saved game data to a developer-operated server. Device backup and restore may be managed by your operating system.

Advertising and consent

MeowTrails uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses (including an approximate location inferred from them), device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to provide advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Collection depends on platform, permissions, consent, and SDK configuration. MeowTrails does not request GPS location access.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages advertising consent where applicable. When available, use Privacy Settings in the game's settings to review or change your choices. On iOS, tracking permission is controlled in system settings; denying tracking does not prevent gameplay. Android provides advertising ID controls in system settings. These choices do not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing.

See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Storage, requests, and contact

Local game data remains on the device until removed or overwritten; operating-system backups may retain copies. Google handles advertising data under its policies, including retention and processing locations. MeowTrails does not offer an account or cloud-save deletion service. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer at the email above. If you email us, your message and address are used to respond to your request.

Audience

MeowTrails is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app or a support request, contact us using the email above.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and revise its effective date.

简体中文

游戏数据

MeowTrails（喵迹） 是一款逻辑解谜游戏，无需注册账号。游戏进度、棋盘状态、教程进度和偏好设置保存在设备上。当前版本不会将这些游戏存档发送到开发者运营的服务器。设备备份与恢复可能由操作系统管理。

广告与同意

MeowTrails（喵迹） 使用 Google AdMob 展示横幅广告。Google 广告 SDK 可能处理 IP 地址（包括由此推断的大致位置）、设备标识符、广告互动、诊断或性能信息，用于广告展示、效果衡量和防止欺诈。实际收集取决于平台、权限、同意选择及 SDK 配置。MeowTrails（喵迹） 不请求 GPS 定位权限。

适用时，Google 用户消息平台管理广告同意。在游戏设置中出现「隐私设置」时，你可以打开它查看或更改选择。iOS 追踪权限可在系统设置中管理，拒绝追踪不影响游玩。Android 系统提供广告 ID 管理选项。这些选择不一定停止所有与广告有关的数据处理。

详情见 Google 隐私政策及 Google 如何使用接入其服务的应用所提供的信息。

保存、请求与联系

本地游戏数据会保留至被移除或覆盖；操作系统备份中可能仍有副本。Google 根据其政策管理广告数据，包括保存期限和处理地点。MeowTrails（喵迹） 没有账号或云存档删除服务。如有隐私问题或相关请求，请通过上方邮箱联系开发者。你发送邮件时，邮件内容和地址用于回复该请求。

受众

MeowTrails（喵迹） 面向一般受众，并非专门面向儿童。如你认为儿童通过本应用或支持请求提供了个人信息，请通过上方邮箱联系我们。

政策更新

应用的数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策及生效日期。
